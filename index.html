Victory!

Two players, three dice, 21 tokens each. Each round, both players roll up to three times, setting aside dice to keep. The player with the higher combination pays the lower the token value of the winner's score. Win by reaching 0 tokens.

Special Combinations ("figures")

  • "421" — 4, 2, 1. Worth 20 points (all tokens minus one). Strongest combo.
  • "Fiches" — Two aces + any other die. Worth the third die's value (2 to 6 points).
  • "Tierce" — Three of a kind. Worth the die value (2 to 6), except 1-1-1 which is worth 7. Loses to "fiches" of the same value.
  • "Suites" — Three consecutive numbers. Worth 2 points. 6-5-4 beats 3-2-1.
  • "Nénette" — 2-2-1. Worth 4 points, but loses to any other 4-point combo.

Other Combos

Worth 1 point. Ranked by highest die, then second, then third (e.g. 6-6-5 beats 5-5-3).

"Égalité"

Same combination, different number of rolls: the player who needed fewer rolls wins and pays the tied value.

"Rampo"

Same combination, same number of rolls. First player chooses:

  • "pour ce qui est" — pay the original tied value.
  • "pour ce qui vient" — both re-roll once; pay the new value.
